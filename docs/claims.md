# Claims

- Mechanism claim: disappearing-goal manipulation requires separating perceptual absence from physical invalidation or task-goal change.
- Evidence claim: the included local benchmark tests hidden, moved, removed, obstructed, changed, substitutable, and cascading disappearing-goal regimes.
- Method claim: goal-validity belief revision improves success without increasing unsafe reaches or false abandonment relative to the strongest non-oracle baseline.
- Scope claim: results support mechanism plausibility in a local executable benchmark, not real-robot deployment.
- Unsupported claim explicitly avoided: no claim of state-of-the-art robot manipulation performance or ICLR-main submission readiness.
