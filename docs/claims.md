# Claims

- Mechanism claim: disappearing-goal manipulation requires separating perceptual absence from physical invalidation, object motion, task-goal change, delayed reappearance, substitute feasibility, and abandonment risk.
- Evidence claim: the v5 local benchmark tests 6 tasks, 8 disappearing-goal regimes, 8 splits, 15 methods, 10 seeds, ablations, stress sweeps, fixed-risk budgets, and negative cases.
- Method claim: `risk_calibrated_goal_belief_revision_v5` improves hard-aggregate success, goal-validity F1, stale-goal pursuit, unsafe reach, false abandonment, calibration, and utility over the strongest non-oracle baseline, `proposed_disappearing_goal_belief_revision_v4`.
- Scope claim: results support mechanism plausibility in a local executable benchmark, not real-robot deployment.
- Unsupported claim explicitly avoided: no claim of state-of-the-art robot manipulation performance or ICLR-main submission readiness.
